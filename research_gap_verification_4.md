4. PNGX Market Capitalisation

Status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED — NEEDS DEFINITION/DATE CHECK

The PNGX About page currently displays:

«Market Capitalization: K138 billion»

However, the same page says PNGX "currently lists 12 companies", while the current Listed Companies page shows 13.

This indicates that the About page may not have been updated consistently.

Therefore:

K138 billion must NOT be presented as the current 4 October 2026 PNGX market capitalisation without establishing its reference date and calculation basis.

There is also an important conceptual issue:

PNGX market capitalisation may include companies with foreign ownership, dual listings or other characteristics that mean total PNGX market capitalisation should not automatically be described as the value of PNG's domestic corporate sector.

Editorial treatment

Until the date and definition are confirmed:

«DO NOT use K138 billion as a current headline statistic.»

If used later, provide:

- valuation date
- source publication date
- definition of market capitalisation
- whether dual-listed/foreign companies are included

Source

PNGX About PNGX:

https://www.pngx.com.pg/about-pngx/

8. Financial Inclusion

Status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED

The National Financial Inclusion Strategy (NFIS) 2023–2027 remains an important policy framework.

Verified findings include:

- financial inclusion is a major national policy priority;
- focus areas include women, rural communities, mobile/digital financial services, financial literacy, consumer protection and data measurement;
- BPNG/CEFI reports that more than 250,000 people have been trained in financial literacy;
- approximately 120,000 of those trained were women;
- a policy objective is to achieve at least 50% of Papua New Guineans in the formal banking network by 2030.

BPNG has also emphasised that simply opening an account does not necessarily mean meaningful financial inclusion.

Important distinction

Financial inclusion is broader than bank-account ownership.

It can involve:

- access;
- affordability;
- usage;
- quality;
- financial literacy;
- consumer protection;
- digital access;
- suitability of financial services.

Editorial treatment

Do not claim:

«"X% of Papua New Guineans are financially included"»

unless a current authoritative source clearly defines and supports that number.

Sources

BPNG — Driving Financial Sector Reform, Inclusion and Stability:

https://www.bankpng.gov.pg/publications/speeches/driving-financial-sector-reform-inclusion-and-stability

BPNG — AFI-related financial inclusion material:

https://www.bankpng.gov.pg/

Status: PARTIALLY VERIFIED

Women's financial inclusion is explicitly identified as a national financial-inclusion priority.

The verified financial-literacy programme evidence indicates that approximately 120,000 women have been included among more than 250,000 people trained.

However, this statistic is a training statistic, not a national measure of women's financial access.

A reliable current national percentage for women's access to formal financial services was not established.

It is acceptable to discuss women's financial inclusion as a policy priority and use the financial-literacy training figures with their correct context.

Do not convert the training figure into a national female financial-access rate.

10. Deposit Protection / Deposit Insurance

Status: NEEDS VERIFICATION

BPNG legislation confirms protection of depositors within the regulatory framework governing authorised financial institutions.

However, the research did not establish a separate, clearly documented deposit-insurance scheme comparable to formal deposit-insurance systems in some other countries.

This distinction is important.

Do not state:

«"PNG has no protection for bank deposits."»

That would be misleading.

Do not state:

«"PNG has a government-backed deposit insurance scheme."»

unless a current authoritative source establishes this.

Editorial treatment

The final book should distinguish:

1. prudential regulation and depositor protection under banking law;
2. supervision of authorised institutions;
3. any separate deposit-insurance or deposit-guarantee arrangement.

This gap requires additional verification before publication if the book intends to discuss deposit insurance specifically.

14. Crypto / Virtual Assets

Status: NEEDS VERIFICATION

BPNG material confirms that it has not licensed virtual-asset service providers or crypto investment platforms and has warned users regarding unlicensed schemes.

This establishes an important regulatory position.

However, the research did not establish a sufficiently comprehensive and current statement covering the complete legal status of:

- cryptocurrency ownership;
- cryptocurrency trading;
- virtual-asset service providers;
- crypto investment platforms;
- taxation;
- AML/CFT obligations;
- licensing requirements;
- enforcement.

Editorial treatment

The book should NOT make a sweeping statement such as:

«"Cryptocurrency is illegal in PNG."»

unless supported by a current authoritative legal source.

Likewise, it should not state that cryptocurrency is fully regulated.

A safer formulation is:

«BPNG has warned the public about unlicensed cryptocurrency and investment schemes and has stated that it has not licensed virtual-asset service providers or crypto investment platforms.»

The precise legal status should be verified again before publication.

- UNRESOLVED

---

20. Do Not Mix These Categories

The final manuscript must clearly distinguish:

Actual

A measured value already observed.

Historical

A measured value from a previous period.

Estimate

A calculated or estimated value.

Forecast

A projected future value.

Budget projection

A government budget assumption/projection.

Target

A policy objective that may not yet have been achieved.

Interpretation

An analytical explanation rather than a measured statistic.

For example:

US$4.4 billion end-2026 reserves

= forecast.

K66.2157 billion public debt

= 2026 Budget projection.

50% formal banking-network participation by 2030

= policy objective/target.

These must not be presented as current actual achievements.

23. Editorial Decision

The project should not be delayed indefinitely waiting for every research gap to be filled.

If a reliable statistic cannot be established after reasonable primary-source research, the preferred approach is:

1. state the underlying concept;
2. use a reliable qualitative explanation;
3. omit the uncertain number;
4. record the unresolved research gap internally.

This is preferable to publishing a precise-looking statistic with uncertain provenance.

---

24. Final Pre-Publication Rule

Immediately before publication, perform one final web verification of all statistics labelled:

- current;
- latest;
- as at 2026;
- 2026 figure;
- present;
- currently;
- today;
- forecast.

Financial markets change continuously. A figure that is correct during research can become outdated before publication.

The final manuscript should therefore favour dated, sourced, transparent statistics over a large quantity of unsupported numerical claims.
