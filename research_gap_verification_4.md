## Additional controlled claims

- IMF growth/inflation projections versus actual results.
- BPNG inflation/reserve projections versus actual outcomes.
- WTO annual export measures versus BPNG quarterly trade statistics.
- Financial-literacy reach of over 250,000 people including around 120,000 women: verify programme, period and counting method.
- Claims that 75% are financially excluded or deposit-account coverage is around 32%: do not use without exact source/date/definition.
- Registered mobile-money accounts are not active people.
- PNGX K138bn and SCPNG US$20.3bn from October 2021 are not interchangeable current figures.

## Recommended verification order

1. Legal/safety-sensitive claims: deposit insurance, virtual assets and broker authorisation.
2. Current market-access facts: PNGX listings, banks and licensed institutions.
3. Market data: capitalisation, turnover, Treasury yields and government securities.
4. Actual macroeconomic results versus forecasts.
5. Superannuation and insurance tables.
6. Inclusion, gender, mobile-money and scam indicators.
7. Record exact source titles, dates, links, tables/pages and limitations in `RESEARCH_MASTER.md`.
