# Decisions

A log of project decisions. Add new decisions in sequence.

## Decision 1 — Book Type

The project is a general-audience financial-markets eBook rather than a formal school textbook.

## Decision 2 — Primary Audience

PNG secondary students and the PNG general public are the primary audiences.

## Decision 3 — Wider Audience

The book should remain useful to readers outside PNG.

## Decision 4 — PNG Context

PNG examples and institutions should be incorporated where useful and properly supported by evidence.

## Decision 5 — Evidence

Important factual claims require appropriate verification.

## Decision 6 — AI Roles

ChatGPT is primarily the planning/research/review agent. Claude is primarily the delegated production agent. The user has final authority.

## Decision 7 — Source of Truth

GitHub is the project source of truth.

## Decision 8 — Controlled Batches

Major project work should be performed in controlled batches with review between stages.

## Decision 9 — Research Master as Research-Control Document

**Status: Proposed (18 October 2026) — takes effect when approved by the user.**

`RESEARCH_MASTER.md` v2.0 is the authoritative research-control document. Original research and handover files are preserved as provenance material and are not deleted, renamed or overwritten.

## Decision 10 — Handling Unverifiable Statistics

**Status: Proposed (18 October 2026) — takes effect when approved by the user.**

The project will not be delayed indefinitely waiting for every statistic. If a reliable figure cannot be established from primary sources after reasonable research, the book states the concept, gives a qualitative explanation, omits the uncertain number, and records the gap internally. Every statistic that is used carries a date, source and one of the standard status labels in `RESEARCH_MASTER.md` (section 3). Forecasts, budget projections and targets are never presented as current actual figures.

## Decision 11 — Final Pre-Publication Verification

**Status: Proposed (18 October 2026) — takes effect when approved by the user.**

Immediately before release, every statistic labelled current, latest, "as at 2026", present, today or forecast is re-verified against its primary source. Policy rates, PNGX listings and market figures are the first items to recheck.
