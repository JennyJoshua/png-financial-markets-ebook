# Workflow

The standard production workflow for the PNG Financial Markets eBook.

**Rule:** A later phase must not be treated as approved merely because the files required for it can technically be created. Progression between phases requires review and approval by the user.

## Phase 1 — Research
Research the financial-market subject broadly and identify relevant PNG context.

## Phase 2 — Research Audit
Identify unsupported claims, missing evidence, outdated information, conflicting information, unclear definitions and research gaps.

## Phase 3 — Research Master
Consolidate reliable research into the project's controlled research reference.

## Phase 4 — Book Architecture
Determine the overall conceptual structure of the book.

## Phase 5 — Detailed Outline
Define chapters and sections.

## Phase 6 — Chapter Specifications
Before drafting, specify for each chapter: purpose, learning/explanatory objectives, concepts, examples, PNG applications, evidence requirements and important terminology.

## Phase 7 — Drafting
Draft chapters in controlled batches.

## Phase 8 — Editorial Review
Check clarity, flow, repetition, audience suitability, consistency and terminology.

## Phase 9 — Fact Checking
Verify factual claims, statistics, dates, institutional names, regulatory claims and citations.

## Phase 10 — Production
Prepare layout, graphics, tables, references and eBook formatting.

## Phase 11 — Publication QA
Check formatting, navigation, links, references, accessibility, rendering, metadata and final file integrity.

## Phase 12 — Release
Prepare the final free downloadable publication.

## Working Cycle

ChatGPT planning/research → Claude handover → Claude creates/edits files → Claude packages files into ZIP → User downloads ZIP → User manually uploads approved files to GitHub → ChatGPT reviews the resulting GitHub project → Next batch is prepared.

Claude cannot be assumed to have repository access, and files must not be assumed to have been uploaded.
