# Project Requirements

## Purpose

Create a free downloadable financial-markets eBook that is accurate, accessible and useful.

## Audience

**Primary**
- PNG secondary students
- PNG general public

**Secondary**
- Wider general audience

## Nature of the Book

The book is:
- educational;
- general audience;
- practical;
- accessible;
- PNG-relevant.

It is not:
- a formal school textbook;
- individualised investment advice;
- a promise of financial success.

## Content Requirements

The eventual book should explain, where appropriate:
- financial markets;
- financial institutions;
- market participants;
- financial instruments;
- investment concepts;
- banking;
- securities;
- shares/equities;
- bonds/government securities;
- foreign exchange;
- insurance;
- superannuation;
- digital/mobile finance;
- financial inclusion;
- risks;
- scams/fraud;
- regulation;
- relevant PNG financial institutions and markets.

The exact chapter structure will be determined later through the research and architecture stages.

## PNG Relevance

The book should meaningfully connect concepts to Papua New Guinea without forcing PNG examples where reliable evidence is unavailable.

## Research and Evidence

Important factual claims require appropriate verification. Statistics must be sourced and dated, and unverified figures must not be presented as established fact.

## Quality

The final book should be:
- accurate;
- evidence-based;
- understandable;
- coherent;
- well structured;
- visually suitable for digital publication;
- properly fact-checked.
