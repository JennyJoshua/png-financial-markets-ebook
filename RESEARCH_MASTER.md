## 2. Editorial Rules

1. Explain financial-market concepts in plain language.
2. Introduce global concepts before applying them to PNG.
3. Use PNG examples wherever they improve understanding.
4. Avoid unnecessary technical jargon; define terms on first use.
5. Do not give personalised investment advice.
6. Do not imply that any investment is guaranteed.
7. Do not imply that diversification eliminates risk.
8. Do not imply that past performance predicts future performance.
9. Clearly distinguish: actual, historical fact, estimate, forecast, budget projection, target, interpretation, unresolved claim.
10. Dates must accompany important statistics.
11. PNG legal and regulatory claims should come from official PNG sources.
12. Do not use an outdated statistic merely because it is widely repeated online.
13. Where current information cannot be verified, mark it NEEDS VERIFICATION.
14. Explain statistics in context rather than presenting unexplained numbers.

**Preferred approach to gaps:** if a reliable statistic cannot be established after reasonable primary-source research, state the concept, give a qualitative explanation, omit the uncertain number, and record the gap internally. A precise-looking number with uncertain provenance is worse than no number.

## 3. Statistic Metadata Rule and Status Labels

Every important PNG statistic used in the ebook must record, where available: value, unit, reference period, publication date, source institution, source document, URL, table/page/section, and status.

**Status labels (use exactly these):**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED — current | Checked against a suitable primary source for the stated date |
| VERIFIED — historical | Correct for a stated past period; not necessarily current |
| VERIFIED — forecast | A published projection, not an observed result |
| VERIFIED — budget projection | A government budget assumption |
| TARGET | A policy objective not yet achieved |
| PARTIALLY VERIFIED | Part of the claim is supported; definition, date or scope is not |
| NEEDS VERIFICATION | Not established; do not publish as fact |
| UNRESOLVED | Contradictory or insufficient evidence |
| NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW | Legal interpretation required |

Examples: a BPNG end-2026 reserves projection is a forecast; K66.2 billion end-2026 debt is a budget projection; 50% formal-banking participation by 2030 is a target. None may be shown as a current actual.

## 5. Core Financial-Market Concepts

Financial markets are systems in which financial claims and assets are issued, bought, sold, exchanged or priced.

**Core functions:** connecting savers and borrowers; allocating capital; price discovery; providing liquidity; transferring and managing risk; transforming maturities; supporting economic activity.

**Four distinctions the book must make clear:** financial markets; financial institutions; financial instruments/assets; financial services.

## 7. BPNG and Monetary Policy

BPNG's primary objective is price stability; broader objectives include financial stability, sustainable growth and development of the financial system. Since the Central Banking Act amendments, policy is set by a five-member Monetary Policy Committee (MPC).

### 7.1 Verified policy settings (checked against BPNG primary documents, 18 October 2026)

| Setting | Value | Meeting / date | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kina Facility Rate (KFR) | 5.0% | MPC 1/2026, 3 March 2026 | S01 | VERIFIED — current at that date |
| Cash Reserve Requirement (CRR) | 9.0% | MPC 1/2026, 3 March 2026 | S01 | VERIFIED — current at that date |
| KFR and CRR | KFR 5.0%, CRR 9.0% | June 2026 | S04 (BPNG homepage "5.00% June 2026"); S07 (Westpac, corroborating) | VERIFIED — current at that date |
| KFR and CRR | KFR 5.0%, CRR 9.0% maintained | MPC 3/2026, 8 September 2026 | S02 | VERIFIED — current at that date |
| Exchange-rate arrangement | Crawl-like arrangement continues; exchange rate is the nominal anchor for inflation | March and September 2026 | S01, S02 | VERIFIED — current |
| Policy-rate transmission | BPNG describes transmission through the banking system as weak | March and September 2026 | S01, S02 | VERIFIED |

### 7.2 Policy history (for context)

| Date | Change | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| May 2024 | KFR raised 2.0% → 2.5%; CRR raised 10% → 11% | S05 | VERIFIED — historical |
| June 2024 | CRR raised to 12% | S06 (BIS-hosted Governor speech) | VERIFIED — historical |
| June 2025 | KFR held at 4.0%; CRR reduced to 10.0% | S03 | VERIFIED — historical |
| Between July 2025 and March 2026 | KFR raised 4.0% → 5.0%; CRR reduced 10.0% → 9.0% | S04 (4.0% shown July 2025); S01 (5.0% / 9.0% by March 2026) | PARTIALLY VERIFIED — the exact meetings for the final step in each rate are not yet confirmed from BPNG statements |

**Note:** do not describe the CRR path as "10% to 9%" on its own. The documented path is 12% (mid-2024) → 10% (June 2025) → 9% (by March 2026).

### 7.3 Plain-language rules for the book

- The KFR is BPNG's signalling rate. Commercial deposit and loan rates do not move automatically or identically with it; BPNG itself describes transmission as weak.
- The CRR is the share of deposits banks must hold with BPNG. A lower CRR releases liquidity; a higher one tightens it.
- The exchange rate matters to households through the kina price of imported fuel, food and goods.
- Policy settings are time-sensitive. Never present a policy rate as permanent, and recheck immediately before publication.

## 14. Financial Inclusion and Digital Finance

The National Financial Inclusion Strategy (NFIS) 2023–2027 is a key source. Priorities: women, rural communities, mobile/digital banking, financial literacy, consumer protection, data measurement.

- BPNG/CEFI: more than 250,000 people have received financial-literacy training, about 120,000 of them women. This is a **training statistic**, not an access rate. VERIFIED.
- Policy objective: at least 50% of Papua New Guineans in the formal banking network by 2030. TARGET.
- BPNG has reported more than 85% of the low-income population live in rural areas and lack access to formal financial services. Keep exact source, date and definition. PARTIALLY VERIFIED.
- Financial inclusion is broader than account ownership: access, affordability, usage, quality, literacy, consumer protection, suitability.
- Current national inclusion rate: no single reliable headline figure (PARTIALLY VERIFIED). Women's national access rate: not established.
- Mobile money: do not equate registered accounts, active users, unique individuals and bank-account ownership. The circulated figure of 1.47 million registered accounts (Q4 2024) must not be used unless the original official source, date and definition are located. NEEDS VERIFICATION.

## 15. Superannuation, Insurance and Deposit Protection

**Superannuation (PARTIALLY VERIFIED).** BPNG's authorised funds: Defence Force Retirement Benefit Fund; Nambawan Super; National Superannuation Fund; Pac Super. BPNG public information indicates, subject to statutory conditions, an employer contribution of 8.4% of base salary and an employee deduction of 6.0%. Do not publish as a universal rule until the statute and fund guidance confirm exactly who it applies to. Aggregate assets, membership and returns still need extraction from the latest BPNG Quarterly Economic Bulletin tables.

**Insurance (PARTIALLY VERIFIED).** The licensed sector and its regulatory structure are confirmed at BPNG's licensed-entities page. Aggregate premiums, assets and penetration are not established; explain the role of insurance without sector-size statistics unless sourced.

**Deposit protection (NEEDS VERIFICATION).** Depositor protection under banking legislation is confirmed; a separate deposit-insurance scheme is not established. Do not say "PNG has no protection for bank deposits," and do not say "PNG has government-backed deposit insurance." Distinguish (1) prudential regulation and depositor protection under banking law, (2) supervision of authorised institutions, (3) any separate deposit-guarantee scheme.

## 16. Crypto, Virtual Assets, Scams and Consumer Protection

**Crypto (NEEDS VERIFICATION / NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW).** Verified BPNG position: it has not licensed virtual-asset service providers or crypto investment platforms, and warns that people dealing with unlicensed schemes do so at their own risk. Do not say crypto is "illegal" or "fully regulated." Safer wording: BPNG has warned the public about unlicensed crypto and investment schemes and has stated that it has not licensed virtual-asset service providers or crypto investment platforms.

**Scams (NEEDS VERIFICATION for prevalence).** BPNG publishes public warnings, and its homepage in mid-2026 carried a notice on an unlicensed "investment" scheme on social media (S04). No reliable national statistic on scam numbers, victims or losses has been established; do not invent or extrapolate one.

**Warning signs to teach:** guaranteed high returns; pressure to invest immediately; requests to send money to unknown persons; unlicensed platforms; fake endorsements; requests for passwords or PINs; unrealistic crypto returns; pyramid or Ponzi-style structures. Check any legal or regulatory claim about scams against current PNG authorities.
