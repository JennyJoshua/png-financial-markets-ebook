# Contributing

Instructions for anyone, human or AI, working on this project.

## General Rules

1. Read the project instructions first.
2. Understand the requirements.
3. Inspect existing material before modifying it.
4. Preserve useful existing work.
5. Avoid unnecessary changes.
6. Use reliable sources.
7. Record uncertainty.
8. Avoid unsupported claims.
9. Work in controlled batches.
10. Clearly report what was changed.
11. Never delete important material without authorisation.
12. Separate research, planning, drafting and verification.

## Additional Rules for AI Agents

- Do not assume missing context.
- Ask for clarification when a major decision is ambiguous.
- Do not fabricate repository state.
- Do not fabricate sources or statistics.
- Do not silently change major project decisions.
