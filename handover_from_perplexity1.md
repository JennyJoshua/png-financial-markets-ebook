# Handover to Perplexity — Research and Verification for the PNG Financial Markets eBook

**Status:** Reconstructed replacement handover  
**Project:** PNG Financial Markets eBook  
**Baseline to update:** 4 October 2026  
**Purpose:** Obtain source-grounded research and verify unresolved claims before accepting the research master.

## Project context

The project is a free downloadable general-audience eBook, especially useful to PNG secondary students and the PNG public. It is not a formal school textbook and must not provide personalised investment advice.

Prioritise Papua New Guinea and authoritative primary sources: BPNG, PNG Treasury, NSO, SCPNG, PNGX, relevant regulators, IMF, World Bank and WTO.

## Research method and output standards

For every important claim, provide:
1. Exact claim or indicator.
2. Value and units, where applicable.
3. Reference period and publication date.
4. Exact source title and direct URL.
5. Page/table/paragraph where available.
6. Whether the figure is actual, provisional, estimate or forecast.
7. Definition, limitations and why it matters.

Open and inspect underlying sources when possible. Do not rely only on search-result snippets. Never invent citations, page numbers, source titles or legal interpretations. If a claim cannot be verified, say so and recommend the next source or authority to consult. Keep direct quotations short and summarise in your own words.

## Priority tasks

### A. PNGX and securities market
- Verify the current PNGX listed-company count and exact list.
- Resolve the discrepancy in the 4 October 2026 baseline: PNGX's About page quoted K138 billion market capitalisation but referred to 12 companies, while the listings page showed 13.
- Establish the date and calculation basis for any current market-capitalisation figure.
- Verify currently authorised brokers through official regulator/exchange sources.
- Research IPOs, market turnover, liquidity, retail participation, trading processes, disclosures and risks.
- Treat SCPNG's US$20.3 billion figure from October 2021 as historical.

### B. Banking and financial institutions
- Verify the current BPNG list of commercial banks, other licensed financial institutions and savings and loan societies.
- Baseline count as at 29 July 2025: seven commercial banks, 13 licensed financial institutions and 18 savings and loan societies. Confirm definitions and current status.
- Verify payment-service-provider status separately from banking licences.

### C. Monetary policy and macroeconomy
- Verify latest KFR and CRR, with exact dates.
- Find latest actual inflation/GDP results and latest forecasts; keep them separate.
- Verify foreign reserves and distinguish current actual levels from end-year projections.
- Verify public debt and debt-to-GDP, identifying actual versus budget projection.
- Confirm the exact IMF wording on PNG's exchange-rate arrangement and current FX-access conditions.

### D. Government securities
- Find current official Treasury bill yield data.
- Record observation/auction date, maturity, yield measure and source table.
- Do not label historical yields around 7.7–8.2% as current without a dated source.
- Explain Treasury bills versus bonds and verify access arrangements in PNG.

### E. Financial inclusion and digital finance
- Identify reliable current national inclusion indicators, definitions and dates.
- Verify or reject claims that 75% of people are financially excluded or deposit-account coverage is around 32%.
- Verify the reported 1.47 million registered mobile-money accounts in Q4 2024; do not equate accounts with active users, unique people or adults.
- Find evidence on women's financial access and distinguish programme reach from national outcomes.
- Research fintech and Digital Kina status only from authoritative, dated sources.

### F. Depositor protection
- Research the current PNG legal and institutional position on depositor protection and deposit insurance.
- Distinguish prudential regulation, depositor rights, lender-of-last-resort arrangements and a formal deposit-insurance scheme.
- Do not infer that no scheme exists merely because a search failed to find one.

### G. Cryptocurrency and virtual assets
- Find current laws, regulations and official BPNG/SCPNG notices, including any licensing requirements.
- Distinguish warnings about unlicensed providers from a categorical prohibition of every crypto-related activity.
- State the precise unresolved legal question if the position cannot be established.

### H. Scams and consumer protection
- Locate official warnings and reporting channels.
- Do not invent national victim counts or loss totals.
- Provide educational warning signs and reliable official contacts.

### I. Superannuation and insurance
- Extract the latest official aggregate assets, membership, contributions, returns or sector statistics only when the source supports them.
- Verify current authorised funds and insurers.
- Check contribution percentages and statutory conditions.
- Do not fill missing data with unsupported estimates.

### J. Trade and commodity dependence
- Verify WTO findings that energy and mineral products represented more than 77% of PNG exports in 2024 and commodity-dependence index was around 97%.
- Verify BPNG March-quarter 2024 figures: K11,334.0 million merchandise exports; K9,958.5 million mineral products including LNG/condensate, around 87.9%.
- Keep annual and quarterly measures distinct.
- Verify the World Bank current-account surplus figure around 15.1% of GDP for 2024 and stated drivers.
